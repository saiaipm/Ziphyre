# Chartered Accountant — Taxation & Compliance

## Company Overview

We are a growing distribution and retail business with operations
across South India, looking to strengthen our finance function as we
scale.

## Role Summary

We are looking for a detail-oriented Chartered Accountant to own
taxation and compliance for the business — GST and TDS end to end,
statutory finalisation, and support during audits. This role reports
to the Finance Manager and works closely with the accounts team on
day-to-day bookkeeping and month-end close.

## Key Responsibilities

- Manage GST compliance: monthly GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B filings, annual
  GSTR-9 reconciliation, and reconciliation of GST input/output
  ledgers against books.
- Manage TDS compliance: quarterly return filing, Form 16A issuance,
  and TDS ledger reconciliation.
- Maintain working knowledge of Direct and Indirect Tax provisions and
  Income Tax provisions relevant to the business.
- Finalise accounts up to Trial Balance and prepare Balance Sheet and
  Profit & Loss statements for management and statutory review.
- Oversee journal entries and maintain the general ledger.
- Perform monthly bank reconciliations across company accounts.
- Assist in preparing monthly MIS reports, financial forecasts and
  budgetary analysis for the finance team.
- Facilitate statutory and internal audits, including preparing
  documentation for auditors.
- Maintain and strengthen internal controls across the accounts
  function.

## Key Job Information

**Location:** Hyderabad (Work from Office)
**Experience:** 2–4 years post-qualification preferred
**Schedule:** 6 days a week

## Requirements

- Qualified Chartered Accountant (ICAI).
- Hands-on experience with Tally is mandatory.
- Strong working knowledge of MS Excel.
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.
- High attention to detail and a commitment to confidentiality and
  ethical standards.
- Strong communication skills, comfortable working directly with
  auditors and cross-functional teams.
